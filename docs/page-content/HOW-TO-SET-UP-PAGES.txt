LE PARFUM SIMPLIFIÉ: SETTING UP YOUR PAGES
==========================================

Page text lives in Shopify admin (Online Store → Pages); the design comes from the theme.
Pick the template shown below for each page. The templates appear in the dropdown once the
new theme is published (or preview them first via Customize → top dropdown → Pages).

BEFORE PUBLISHING ANY DRAFT TEXT
  • Replace every [BRACKETED PLACEHOLDER] with your real details.
  • The terms are plain-English drafts based on what your site already says. They are not
    legal advice. Ask a solicitor or a service such as a legal template provider to check
    them, especially the Terms of Service and Membership Terms.

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGES THAT ALREADY EXIST: just change the template
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. About            (/pages/about-1)          → Template: lps-about
   The new About layout writes its own text (from your current About page), so the
   page's existing body text is not shown. Edit the wording in Customize.
2. Contact          (/pages/contact)          → Template: lps-contact
3. Refund policy    (/pages/refund-policy)    → Template: lps-policy   (keeps your existing text)
4. Premium Learning (/pages/premium-learning) → Template: lps-premium-learning
5. Perfume development (/pages/perfume-development) → Template: lps-perfume-development
6. Software         (/pages/software)         → Template: lps-software

------------------------------------------------------------------
NEW PAGES TO CREATE: Online Store → Pages → Add page
------------------------------------------------------------------
For each one: type the Title, then click the "<>" (Show HTML) button in the content editor,
paste the contents of the matching .html file, switch back, and check it reads correctly.
Under "Search engine listing", check the URL handle matches the one below exactly.

7. Title: FAQ
   URL handle: faq
   Content: leave empty (the FAQ layout has its own questions; edit them in Customize)
   Template: lps-faq

8. Title: Delivery and access
   URL handle: delivery-and-access
   Content: delivery-and-access.html
   Template: lps-policy

9. Title: Membership terms
   URL handle: membership-terms
   Content: membership-terms.html
   Template: lps-policy

------------------------------------------------------------------
TERMS OF SERVICE: goes in Settings, not Pages
------------------------------------------------------------------
10. Settings → Policies → Terms of service → paste terms-of-service.html (use the HTML view)
    → Save. Shopify then shows it at /policies/terms-of-service and links it at checkout.
    While there, also fill in "Contact information" (your business name, address, email):
    UK law expects online sellers to show this.
