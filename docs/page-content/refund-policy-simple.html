The simple version of our refund policy.

Course and software (digital)

  • You get access straight away.
  • At checkout, you agree to start straight away. This means you lose the usual 14-day right to cancel once access begins.
  • So refunds aren't usually possible after you have access.
  • If something doesn't work or isn't as described, contact us. We'll fix it or refund you.

Physical products

  • You can cancel within 14 days of receiving your order.
  • Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
  • You pay return postage, unless the item is faulty, damaged or wrong.

Faulty or wrong item?

Contact us as soon as possible. We may ask for photos. We'll replace, repair or refund it.

Getting your refund

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few days to show it.

Your legal rights under UK law are not affected.

Contact us with any questions.
